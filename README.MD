# UK Road Safety Data Analysis

An analysis of UK government road casualty statistics using the Department for Transport's 2024 dataset, covering collisions, casualties, and vehicle involvement across Great Britain.

## Overview

This project explores patterns in road safety data to identify where, when, and how serious road casualties occur. The analysis draws on three linked datasets — collisions, casualties, and vehicles — to surface insights around high-risk conditions, vulnerable road users, and geographic hotspots.

## Key findings

- Collision frequency peaks during weekday commuting hours and drops sharply at weekends
- Urban areas account for the majority of casualties, but rural roads produce a disproportionate share of fatal outcomes
- Young drivers (17–24) are significantly over-represented in serious and fatal collisions relative to their share of licence holders
- Motorcyclists and cyclists face the highest casualty rates per journey among all vehicle categories
